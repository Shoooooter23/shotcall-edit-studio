🎬 Edit Studio

Sports highlights & motorcycle cinematic edits • Private, on-device MVP

1. Choose your footage

If you only see Files, tap the menu or Browse / Photos option in Android’s picker. In Google Photos, you can also open a video and download it to your device first. This app cannot directly list your entire Google Photos account.

Test with a short clip first (ideally under 60 seconds).

2. Choose your edit

Motion-based selection is automatic, but it does not yet recognize specific plays, motorcycles or goals.

3. Export your edit

This free prototype exports a silent WebM video using your browser's recording engine. Some Android Gallery and social apps may require MP4 conversion. Music, original audio, captions and speed ramps are future features.

Choose a video to begin.

📱 Add to home screen

First publish this folder to a free HTTPS website (see README). Then open that site in Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen → Install (or Create shortcut). If the menu is missing, check that you are viewing the HTTPS site in a full browser tab, not an HTML file from Downloads or an in-app browser.
